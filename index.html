Infrastructure Priority

DPWH (Public Works) receives the largest departmental budget at PHP 997.9M, indicating strong government focus on infrastructure development.

NCR Dominance

75.8% of the budget goes directly to Local Government Units through tobacco tax shares and national wealth utilization.
